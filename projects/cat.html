Cornell Assistive Technologies

CAT Projects

I'm an engineer on the Cornell Assistive Technologies (CAT) project team. I previously worked on the Tactile Coding Blocks (TCB) project, creating 3D-printable code blocks as a resource for students with visual impairments to learn how to code. During this project, I worked with the team to design the actual CAD models of the blocks, which was challenging and a lot of fun! Our constant iterations of the blocks showed me the importance of establishing clear goals and guidelines.They certainly streamlined our design process and helped our team increase our productivity and consistency across designs. It was incredible hearing feedback on our designs based off several rounds of user testing and feedback. Although TCB has now graduated from CAT, we have several exciting new projects this year, and I'm part of two: the Gamifying PT project and the DROVE Wheelchair project. Both have incredible teams and ideas, and I'm excited to see where we take them! For more information on CAT, here is our website!

My favorite part of being on CAT is our annual GoBabyGo event, which I've dedicated an entire page to, so I won't go into too much detail here. I'll just say that some of my favorite memories of my time at Cornell come from the event, and it is easily what I believe to be some of my most impactful work.
