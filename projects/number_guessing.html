Number Guessing Game

Background

The number guessing game was my team's final project for our Embedded Systems course at Cornell. It was a cumulation of the techniques and skills we learned in the course, including communication protocols (in our game's case UART), interrupt service routines (ISRs), and parsing through documentation. For more information on the course, please see my Courses page.
